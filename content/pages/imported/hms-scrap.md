## HMS Scrap Overview

**[HMS Scrap](https://scraplocal.co.uk/sell/scrap-metal/ferrous/cast-iron)** stands for Heavy Melting Scrap and generally refers to heavy, predominantly ferrous steel and iron materials prepared for recycling. It can originate from construction, demolition, manufacturing, engineering and industrial activities.

Typical materials include heavy steel sections, plates, beams, pipes, structural components and other substantial ferrous metal pieces.

## Recycling Process

**It** is collected, inspected and sorted before being prepared for recycling. Large pieces may be cut or sheared into manageable sizes, while unwanted materials can be separated from the ferrous metal stream.

Prepared material can then be supplied for further processing and use as a secondary raw material in appropriate metal-production applications.

## Sorting and Grading,
