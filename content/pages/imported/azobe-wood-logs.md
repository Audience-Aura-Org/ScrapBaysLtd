## Azobe Wood Logs

[Azobe Wood Logs](https://atozwoodcompany.com/product/azobe-wood-logs/) are dense tropical hardwood timber products known for their exceptional hardness, durability, natural resistance, and suitability for demanding outdoor and heavy-duty applications. Azobe is commonly associated with _Lophira alata_, an African hardwood species also known as Ekki.

The timber is particularly valued where resistance to moisture, wear, and demanding environmental conditions is required. Depending on the intended use, Azobe timber can be processed into beams, boards, sleepers, structural components, marine products, and other heavy-duty timber materials.

Because Azobe is an African tropical hardwood, responsible sourcing and legal timber documentation are important considerations for commercial buyers.

## What Are [Azobe Wood Logs](https://www.boogaerdthout.nl/en/wood-type/azobe/)?

Azobe Wood Logs are round timber sections obtained from Azobe or Ekki trees. The wood is exceptionally dense and hard compared with many other commercial timber species.

Its combination of strength, durability, and resistance to demanding outdoor conditions makes it suitable for specialist construction and infrastructure applications. The exact characteristics of each log depend on its origin, grade, dimensions, moisture content, and quality.

## Appearance and Grain of Azobe Wood Logs

Azobe generally has a dark reddish-brown to deep brown appearance. The colour can become darker with exposure and finishing.

The grain is commonly interlocked or irregular, while the texture is generally coarse to medium. The dense structure contributes to the timber’s distinctive heavy and solid feel.
