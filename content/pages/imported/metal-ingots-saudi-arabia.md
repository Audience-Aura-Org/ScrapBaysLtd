## Import Requirements for Saudi Arabia

Commercial shipments into Saudi Arabia should be checked against the applicable customs classification and import requirements before dispatch. ZATCA states that importers may need documents including a commercial invoice, bill of lading and certificate of origin, with additional documents depending on the nature of the goods.

Saudi Arabia’s integrated customs tariff uses the GCC tariff structure at the 12-digit level, so the correct HS classification should be confirmed for each product.

For commercial imports, buyers should work with their customs broker or relevant Saudi authorities to confirm the current requirements for the exact metal product.
