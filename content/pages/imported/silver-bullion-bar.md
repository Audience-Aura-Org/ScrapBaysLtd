## Silver Bullion Bar Purity

Purity is an important characteristic of a [**Silver Bullion Bar**](/product/999-fine-silver-bar-bullion/) because it indicates the proportion of silver contained in the material.

For example, silver with a fineness of **999** contains 999 parts silver per 1,000 parts of material, subject to the applicable assay standard.

Common silver bullion purity descriptions include:

-   999 fine silver
-   999.9 fine silver
-   High-purity silver
-   Fine silver
-   Refined silver

Buyers should verify the stated purity using appropriate documentation and professional testing when required.
