## Honey Brass Scrap Recycling Process

Collected **[Honey Brass Scrap](/product/aluminum-ubc-scrap/)** is inspected and sorted to separate brass from steel, plastics, rubber and other unwanted materials. Suitable material can then be processed and prepared for further recycling.

The recovered brass can be remelted and used as a secondary raw material for manufacturing new metal products.
