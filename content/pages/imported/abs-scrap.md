## ABS Scrap: Complete Guide to Recycling & Applications

**[ABS Scrap](https://www.jbscrap.com/product/abs-plastic-scrap-acrylonitrile-butadiene-styrene-scrap/index.html)** is discarded Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene plastic collected from electronics, automotive parts, appliances, and industrial products. It can be sorted and processed for reuse in plastic manufacturing.

## Different Types

Common **Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene Scrap** includes electronic housings, automotive components, appliance parts, plastic sheets, and manufacturing offcuts. Materials may vary by colour, size, grade, and cleanliness.

## Recycling Process

**[ABS Scrap](https://www.wastetrade.com/materials/abs-recycling)** is normally collected, sorted, separated from unwanted materials, shredded, washed when necessary, dried, ground, and screened. Good sorting helps create a consistent recycled feedstock.

## Applications

Recycled [**ABS Scrap**](/product/magnesium-alloy-ingots/) can be used for electronic housings, automotive components, appliance parts, tools, fittings, containers, and other moulded plastic products.

## Quality Factors

The quality of **ABS Scrap** depends on polymer purity, colour, contamination, moisture, particle size, additives, and source. Clean and well-separated material is generally preferred by recyclers.

## Benefits of Recycling

Recycling **Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene** **Scrap** helps reduce plastic waste, conserve raw materials, lower disposal volumes, and support a circular plastics economy.

When trading **Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene Scrap**, buyers should consider grade, color, cleanliness, contamination, moisture, particle size, quantity, and packaging.

## Sustainability

Using this material as a recycled feedstock helps recover useful plastic from discarded products and reduces reliance on virgin materials. Proper collection and sorting support efficient material recovery.

**ABS Scrap** is a useful recyclable engineering plastic with applications across electronics, automotive, appliances, and industrial manufacturing. Proper processing and quality control can improve its recycling potential.
