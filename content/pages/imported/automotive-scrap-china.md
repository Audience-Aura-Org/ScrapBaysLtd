## Automotive Recycling China

**Automotive Recycling China** involves the recovery, separation and processing of useful materials from end-of-life vehicles and automotive components.

China requires end-of-life vehicle collection and dismantling activities to be carried out by appropriately qualified enterprises. Regulations also provide for certain major assemblies to enter authorised remanufacturing channels when they meet the applicable conditions, while other unsuitable assemblies are directed to metal recycling.

China also has current technical standards covering the removal and reuse of parts from end-of-life vehicles, including GB/T 44727-2024.
