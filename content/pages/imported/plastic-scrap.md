## Plastic Scrap for Recycling and Industrial Use

**[Plastic Scrap](https://www.gkrecycling.co.uk/what-we-buy.html)** includes recyclable plastic materials recovered from manufacturing, commercial, industrial and post-consumer sources. Different plastic grades can be sorted, processed and prepared for recycling or further manufacturing applications.

Available materials may include HDPE, LDPE, PET, PVC, ABS, PP and other plastic grades, depending on availability, specification and source.

## Types of [Plastic Scrap](/product/weee-scrap/)

### HDPE Plastic Scrap

HDPE plastic scrap is commonly recovered from containers, bottles, drums, industrial packaging and other products. It can be supplied as sorted scrap, regrind or other prepared forms depending on the material.

### LDPE [Plastic Scrap](/product/abs-computer-scrap/)

LDPE plastic scrap can include film, bags, sheets, packaging materials and industrial film. Clean and sorted LDPE materials can be suitable for mechanical recycling and the production of recycled plastic products.

### PET Plastic Scrap

PET scrap includes discarded bottles, containers and other PET-based materials. PET can be processed into flakes, regrind and other recycled feedstocks.

### PVC Scrap

PVC scrap can originate from pipes, profiles, window and door components, cables and other applications. Material condition and composition should be assessed before recycling.

### ABS Plastic Scrap

ABS scrap is found in electronic housings, automotive components, appliances and other manufactured products. Sorted ABS can be processed into recycled material for suitable applications.

### PP Plastic Scrap

PP plastic scrap can include packaging, containers, automotive components and industrial products. Proper sorting by polymer type can improve processing efficiency.

## Plastic Scrap Processing

Plastic materials may be sorted according to polymer type, colour and quality before being processed. Depending on the material, processing can include sorting, separation, cleaning, shredding, grinding and pelletising.

Proper preparation can help recyclers produce consistent recycled feedstock for manufacturing.

## Applications,

Recycled plastic materials can be used in a range of manufacturing and recycling applications, including:

-   Plastic packaging
-   Containers
-   Pipes and profiles
-   Automotive components
-   Industrial products
-   Household products
-   Recycled plastic compounds
-   Plastic sheets and moulded products

The suitability of each material depends on its polymer type, grade, contamination and processing requirements.

## Global Plastic Scrap Supply

**Plastic refuse** can be traded internationally for recycling and material recovery. Buyers should confirm the material classification, quality, destination requirements and applicable environmental regulations before arranging shipments.

International movements of plastic waste can be subject to Basel Convention requirements. The applicable controls depend on the type and condition of the plastic waste and whether it meets the relevant criteria for environmentally sound recycling.
