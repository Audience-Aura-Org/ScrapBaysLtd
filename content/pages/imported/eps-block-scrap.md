## EPS Block Scrap for Recycling and Manufacturing

[EPS Block Scrap](https://elliscombefarmlilited.com/product/buy-eps-block-scrap/) is recovered expanded polystyrene material generated from manufacturing processes, packaging operations, construction activities, insulation products, and other commercial sources. EPS, commonly known as expanded polystyrene, is a lightweight cellular plastic material consisting primarily of polystyrene beads expanded with air.

Recycling [**EPS Block Scrap**](https://ivorypharinc.com/eps-block.html) provides an opportunity to recover usable polystyrene material and redirect it into manufacturing and recycling streams. Depending on its condition, grade, cleanliness, and form, EPS scrap can be compacted, processed, granulated, or recycled into new plastic products.

## What Is EPS Block Scrap?

EPS Block Scrap consists of unwanted, surplus, off-cut, damaged, or recovered expanded polystyrene blocks. These blocks are typically lightweight and contain a high proportion of trapped air, giving EPS its characteristic low density.

Sources of [**EPS Block Scrap**](/product/pet-flakes/) can include insulation manufacturing, packaging production, construction sites, protective packaging, and industrial processing operations.

The material may be supplied as large blocks, off-cuts, production waste, or sorted pieces.

## Recycling

Recycling **EPS Block Scrap** can involve several processing stages depending on the desired end product. Clean post-industrial material may be sorted and processed more easily than heavily contaminated post-consumer material.

Typical recycling processes can include:

1.  Collection
2.  Sorting
3.  Inspection
4.  Removal of contaminants
5.  Compaction or size reduction
6.  Grinding or densification
7.  Reprocessing
8.  Manufacturing into new products

The appropriate recycling method depends on the material’s composition, cleanliness, density, and intended end use.
