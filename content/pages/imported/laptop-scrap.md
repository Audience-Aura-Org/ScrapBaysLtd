**[Laptop Scrap](https://www.repairprice.co.uk/e-waste-prices/laptop-circuit-board-recycling-prices)** comes from discarded, obsolete, damaged, or unwanted laptops collected for electronic recycling. These devices contain circuit boards, batteries, screens, plastics, aluminum, copper, and other recyclable materials.

## What Is Laptop Scrap?

**[Laptop Scrap](https://www.exe.co.uk/blog/recycle-laptop-for-cash)** refers to used laptops and related components collected for recovery and recycling. It can include complete devices, damaged machines, dismantled parts, and obsolete computer equipment.

## Types,

Common categories include:

-   Used laptops
-   Damaged laptops
-   Old and obsolete laptops
-   Laptop motherboards
-   Laptop hard drives
-   Laptop RAM
-   Laptop screens
-   Laptop components

Different brands, models, and generations contain varying materials and component designs.

## Materials in Laptop Scrap

It can contain aluminum, copper, steel, plastics, glass, circuit boards, and electronic components. Some circuit boards and connectors may also contain small quantities of precious metals such as gold, silver, and palladium.

The actual composition depends on the laptop model, manufacturer, age, and component configuration.

## Laptop Scrap Recycling

Recycling **it** generally involves collection, sorting, dismantling, component separation, and material recovery. Suitable recycling facilities can process circuit boards, metals, plastics, and other components through appropriate recovery systems.

Batteries and components requiring specialist handling should be separated and managed through suitable recycling channels.

## Laptop Scrap Value

The value of **[Laptop Scrap](/product/used-mobile-phone/)** depends on factors such as model, condition, quantity, component content, board grade, completeness, and current market conditions.

Sorted and clearly classified material can be easier to assess than mixed electronic equipment. Larger quantities may require inspection and grading.

## Benefits of Recycling,

Responsible recycling helps recover useful materials from unwanted computers while reducing electronic waste. Proper processing can conserve resources, reduce unnecessary disposal, and support a circular electronics industry.
