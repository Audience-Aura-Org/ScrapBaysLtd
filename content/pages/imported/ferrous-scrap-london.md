## Ferrous Scrap Materials

### HMS Scrap

HMS Scrap, or Heavy Melting Steel, can include heavy steel structures, plates, beams, pipes and other steel materials depending on the applicable grade and preparation.

### Cast Iron Scrap

Cast Iron Scrap consists of recyclable cast iron components and products. It can be processed as a secondary raw material for suitable foundry and metal recycling applications.

### Used Rails

Used Rails are steel railway track materials that can be recovered and processed for recycling and steel production according to their condition and specification.

### Steel Scrap

Steel Scrap can originate from manufacturing, construction, fabrication, industrial equipment and other commercial sources. Different grades may require different preparation and processing methods.

### Steel Tire Wire Scrap

Steel Tire Wire Scrap is recovered from tyre-processing operations and contains recyclable steel wire that can be separated and processed for metal recovery.

### Brake Drum Scrap

Brake Drum Scrap commonly consists of cast iron components that can be recovered and processed as ferrous material.

## Ferrous Scrap Processing

Ferrous materials can undergo different processing stages depending on their physical characteristics and intended application.

### Sorting

Different ferrous grades and materials can be separated to improve consistency.

### Cutting

Large steel structures and components can be cut into manageable sizes for handling, transportation and further processing.

### Shredding

Selected ferrous materials can be shredded to reduce their size and assist with material separation.

### Baling

Certain prepared steel scrap products can be compressed into bales for storage and transportation.

### Melting

Processed ferrous scrap can be used as a secondary raw material in suitable steelmaking and foundry operations.
