## Radiator Scrap Overview

**[Radiator Scrap](https://www.londonscrapmetalrecycling.com/metal-recycling/)** is recyclable metal material recovered from vehicle radiators, cooling systems, air-conditioning equipment and industrial machinery. Depending on the type, radiators may contain copper, aluminum and other metal components.

Proper sorting and preparation allow valuable metals to be recovered efficiently.

## What Is Radiator Scrap?

**[Radiator Scrap](https://westmidlandsmetals.co.uk/product-category/scrap-metal-prices/)** generally consists of used or surplus radiators that are no longer required for their original application. Common varieties include copper radiators, aluminum radiators and copper-aluminum mixed radiators.

The material can be sorted according to metal composition, condition and cleanliness before recycling.

## Radiator Scrap Recycling Process

Collected **[Radiator Scrap](https://www.kasmetal.com/radiator-recycling/)** is inspected and sorted according to its metal composition and condition. Where required, unwanted attachments and contaminants are removed before the material is prepared for processing.

Copper and aluminium can then be separated and recovered for use as secondary raw materials.

## Sorting and Grading

Effective sorting helps distinguish copper radiators, aluminium radiators and mixed-metal units. Clean and properly prepared material can improve handling and downstream recycling efficiency.

## Sources

Radiators can be recovered from cars, trucks, buses, air-conditioning systems, industrial machinery, cooling equipment and manufacturing operations.

## Benefits of Radiator Recycling

Recycling radiators helps recover valuable copper and aluminium while reducing waste. Recovered metals can re-enter manufacturing processes, supporting resource conservation and the circular economy.
