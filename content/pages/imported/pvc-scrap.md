## What Is PVC Scrap?

**[PVC Scrap](/product/hdpe-blue-drum-regrind/)** is discarded polyvinyl chloride material collected from construction, manufacturing, packaging, plumbing, electrical, and other industrial sources. It can be processed and recycled into new products, helping reduce plastic waste and conserve raw materials.

## Different Types

Common **PVC** waste includes pipes, window and door profiles, cable insulation, sheets, flooring, hoses, fittings, and production offcuts. Material can be rigid or flexible depending on its original application.

## PVC Waste Recycling Process

The recycling of it generally involves collection, sorting, removal of contaminants, size reduction, washing when required, drying, grinding, and screening. Proper separation helps produce a more consistent recycled feedstock.

## Applications,

Recycled **PVC Scrap** can be used to manufacture pipes, profiles, flooring products, cable components, sheets, mats, construction materials, and other plastic products. The application depends on material grade and cleanliness.

## Quality Factors

The value and usability of **PVC Scrap** depend on polymer type, colour, contamination, moisture, additives, particle size, and whether the material is rigid or flexible. Clean, well-sorted material is generally preferred by recyclers.

## Benefits of Recycling,

Recycling **it** helps reduce landfill waste, recover useful polymer, conserve resources, and support a circular plastics economy. It can also provide manufacturers with recycled material for suitable production processes.

## Sustainability

Using **[PVC Scrap](https://www.simplaspvcrecycling.co.uk/)** as a recycled feedstock helps extend the useful life of PVC materials and reduces the demand for virgin polymer. Effective collection and sorting are important for achieving better recycling results.

**PVC Scrap** provides a valuable source of recyclable plastic for a wide range of manufacturing applications. Proper sorting, processing, quality control, and storage can improve its usability and support sustainable material recovery.
