## Recycling,

Recycling **[Fishnet Scrap](/product/ldpe-film-roll-scrap/)** normally begins with collection and sorting. Nets may then be inspected and prepared for mechanical recycling or another suitable recovery process.

A typical preparation process may include:

1.  Collection
2.  Sorting by polymer type
3.  Removal of foreign materials
4.  Cleaning where required
5.  Cutting or size reduction
6.  Shredding
7.  Further processing or granulation
8.  Reuse as recycled polymer feedstock

The appropriate process depends on the polymer, contamination level, moisture, and intended recycled product.

## Nylon Fishnet Scrap

Nylon or polyamide fishing nets can be valuable recycling materials when collected and sorted appropriately. Nylon is a thermoplastic polymer that can be mechanically processed into recycled material for suitable manufacturing applications.

**Fishnet Scrap** made from nylon should be kept separate from incompatible polymers whenever possible to maintain material consistency.

## Clean Fishnet Scrap

Clean and properly sorted material is generally more suitable for recycling. Excessive contamination from marine debris, ropes, metals, oils, organic matter, sand, or other materials can affect processing and final product quality.

Before supplying **Fishnet Scrap**, unwanted foreign materials should be removed where practical and the polymer type should be clearly identified.
