## Lead Scrap Overview

**[Lead Scrap](https://mohmet.co.uk/lead.html)** is a recyclable non-ferrous metal material recovered from used products, industrial components and manufacturing processes. It can be collected, sorted and prepared for responsible recycling and metal recovery.

## What Is [Lead Scrap](https://milledleadsupplies.co.uk/recycled-scrap-lead/)?

**It** consists of discarded or surplus lead-containing materials that can be recovered and processed. Common forms include lead sheets, pipes, plates, cable sheathing and suitable industrial materials.

Because lead is a hazardous material, collection, handling and recycling should be carried out using appropriate environmental and safety procedures.

## Types of [Lead Scrap](https://www.jbscrap.com/product-category/lead-scrap/index.html)

**It** may include:

-   Lead cable sheathing
-   Lead flashing
-   Lead industrial components
-   Lead manufacturing offcuts
-   Recovered lead materials
