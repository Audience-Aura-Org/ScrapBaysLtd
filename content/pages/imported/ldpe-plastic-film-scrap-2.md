## LDPE Plastic Film Scrap: An Overview

L[DPE Plastic Film Scrap](/about-us/) consists of low-density polyethylene film generated from manufacturing, packaging, agricultural, commercial, and industrial activities. Depending on its source, LDPE film can include production offcuts, packaging film, stretch film, shrink film, and other suitable polyethylene film materials.

Clean, sorted material is generally preferred by recyclers because it can help improve processing efficiency and the quality of the resulting recycled material.

## What Is LDPE Plastic Film Scrap?

LDPE stands for **Low-Density Polyethylene**, a flexible thermoplastic known for its lightweight structure, durability, and resistance to moisture. LDPE is commonly manufactured into films, bags, sheets, packaging materials, and protective coverings.

LDPE Plastic Film Scrap can be collected and prepared for recycling rather than being discarded. After sorting and processing, suitable LDPE film can be converted into recycled plastic feedstock for manufacturing.

## Types of LDPE Plastic Film Scrap

Different sources can produce different types and grades of LDPE film scrap. Common examples include:

-   Clean LDPE film scrap
-   Clear LDPE film
-   Natural LDPE film
-   Coloured LDPE film
-   Stretch film
-   Packaging film
-   Shrink film
-   Industrial LDPE film
-   Manufacturing and production offcuts
-   Post-industrial LDPE film scrap

The exact composition, colour, cleanliness, moisture content, and contamination level can vary depending on the source.

## Clean and Sorted LDPE Film Scrap

**Clean LDPE Plastic Film Scrap** is particularly valuable for recycling because reduced contamination can make sorting, washing, shredding, and extrusion more efficient.

Before recycling, [LDPE film](/ldpe-plastic-film-scrap/) may be inspected and sorted according to factors such as:

-   Material type
-   Colour
-   Film thickness
-   Cleanliness
-   Moisture content
-   Contamination
-   Packaging method
-   Bale density
-   Quantity available

Proper sorting helps recyclers select suitable material for their processing requirements.

## LDPE Plastic Film Scrap Recycling

The recycling process for L[DPE Plastic Film Scrap](/product/hdpe-milk-bottle-scrap/) normally involves several stages. Depending on the material and recycling facility, these can include collection, sorting, baling, shredding, washing, drying, agglomeration, extrusion, and pelletising.

Once processed, recycled LDPE can be used as a feedstock for manufacturing various plastic products. Recycling LDPE film can also help recover useful polymer material from industrial and commercial waste streams.
