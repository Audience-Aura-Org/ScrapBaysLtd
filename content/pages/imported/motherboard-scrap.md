## Motherboard Scrap: Recycling & Metal Recovery

[Motherboard Scrap](https://www.awarefiners.co.uk/computer-recycling.php) comes from computers, laptops, servers, networking equipment, and other electronic devices. These used circuit boards contain recyclable materials such as copper, aluminium, tin, iron, plastics, and electronic components.

## What do you know of it?

[Motherboard Scrap](https://www.escrapwastedisposal.co.uk/pricing.php) refers to obsolete, damaged, surplus, or recovered electronic mainboards collected for recycling. The boards can vary in grade, size, design, component density, and material composition.

## Metals in [Motherboard Scrap](https://ewastedirect.co.uk/)

it can contain copper and other recyclable metals. Some boards also contain small amounts of precious metals such as gold, silver, or palladium in selected connectors, contacts, and electronic components. The actual content depends on the board type and grade.

## Recycling

Recycling typically involves collection, dismantling, sorting, grading, and material separation. Suitable recycling facilities can recover metals from processed circuit boards and send valuable fractions for further refining.

## Benefits of Recycling

Responsible recycling helps reduce electronic waste while recovering useful materials from obsolete equipment. It can support resource conservation, reduce landfill disposal, and contribute to a circular economy.

## Value

The value of it depends on its grade, source, quantity, condition, component density, metal content, sorting, and current recycling-market conditions. Professional inspection and testing can provide a more accurate assessment.
