## Gold Bullion Bar for Investment and Precious Metal Markets

A **[Gold Bullion Bar](https://www.jmbullion.com/gold/gold-bars/)** is a refined gold product produced in a standardized bar or ingot form and valued primarily according to its gold purity, weight, market price, and authenticity. Gold bullion is widely recognized as a store of value and is traded through established precious-metal markets around the world.

Gold bars are available in different sizes and specifications, ranging from small bars suitable for individual investors to larger bars used by professional traders, institutions, refineries, and bullion dealers.

## What Is a [Gold Bullion Bar](https://www.thesilvermountain.nl/en/buy-gold/gold-bars)?

A **[Gold Bullion Bar](https://www.bullionvault.com/gold-guide/gold-bars)** is a quantity of refined gold manufactured into a recognizable bar format. Depending on the producer and market, bars may be cast or minted and can carry markings identifying their weight, fineness, manufacturer, serial number, or other authentication information.

Investment-grade gold is commonly associated with high purity, including 24-karat gold and fineness levels such as 999 or 999.9, although specifications vary by product.

## Gold Bullion Bar Purity

Purity is one of the most important characteristics of a **Gold Bullion Bar**. It indicates the proportion of gold contained in the bar.

For example, a fineness of **999.9** indicates 999.9 parts of gold per 1,000 parts of material, subject to the applicable assay and product standard.

Common descriptions include:

-   24K gold
-   999 fine gold
-   999.9 fine gold
-   High-purity gold
-   Investment-grade gold

Buyers should always verify the stated purity through appropriate documentation and professional testing where required.
