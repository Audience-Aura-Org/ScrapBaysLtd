### What Is Manganese Steel Scrap?

**[Manganese Steel Scrap](https://mbmetals.com/scrap-materials/scrap-manganese-steel/)** is discarded alloy steel containing manganese, valued for its toughness, strength, and resistance to impact and abrasion. It is commonly recovered from heavy-duty industrial equipment.

### Different Types

Common [**Manganese alloy steel**](https://rmcpolska.pl/en/purchase-of-manganese-steel/) includes crusher liners, wear plates, mining equipment parts, railway components, castings, and industrial machinery components.

### Properties,

This material offers excellent toughness and wear resistance, making **Manganese alloy steel** suitable for demanding industrial applications. Its composition varies according to the original steel grade.

### Recycling Process

The recycling of **Manganese alloy steel** generally involves collection, sorting, inspection, cutting, size reduction, and preparation for steelmaking. Proper separation helps maintain material quality.

### Industrial Applications

Recycled **Manganese alloy steel** can be used as ferrous feedstock for steelmaking and other suitable metal-processing applications.

### Quality Factors

The value of **[Manganese Steel Scrap](/product/tantalite-ore/)** depends on manganese content, steel composition, cleanliness, contamination, dimensions, and overall condition.
