## What Are [Wenge Wood Logs](https://exotichardwoodsukltd.com/wood-species/wenge/)?

Wenge Wood Logs are round timber sections obtained from wenge-producing hardwood trees. The name wenge is primarily associated with _Millettia laurentii_, although accurate botanical identification is important when purchasing commercial timber.

## Processing Wenge Wood Logs

Wenge Wood Logs can be processed through several specialist timber-processing stages:
