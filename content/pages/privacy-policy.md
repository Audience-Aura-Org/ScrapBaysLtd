This policy explains how **Scrap Bay Limited** ("we", "us", "our") collects and uses personal data when you use **scrapbaysltd.com** or contact us. We are the data controller for this information. If you have any questions, email [info@scrapbaysltd.com](mailto:info@scrapbaysltd.com).

## Information we collect

We only collect what we need to deal with your business enquiry or order:

- **Contact and company details** you enter in our forms: company name, contact name, email address, phone number and country.
- **Enquiry and order details**: the products, quantities and requirements you send us, billing address, VAT or company registration number and preferred payment method.
- **Scrap material offers**: material type, estimated quantity, location, any message and any photos you upload.
- **Correspondence** you send us by email or phone.
- **Technical data**: our hosting provider records standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) for security and to prevent abuse. We use your IP address to rate-limit form submissions.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Your quote list and wishlist are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and are not sent to us until you submit a form.

## How we use it

- To respond to quote requests and scrap material offers, prepare quotations and negotiate terms.
- To process and fulfil orders, issue invoices and arrange inspection, shipping and payment.
- To send you confirmations and status updates about your enquiry or order.
- To keep business, tax and accounting records as required by law.
- To protect our website and customers from fraud, spam and misuse.

## Legal bases

We process personal data under the UK GDPR on the basis of:

- **Contract** — taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and performing a contract with you or your company.
- **Legitimate interests** — running and securing our trading business and responding to business enquiries.
- **Legal obligation** — keeping records required by tax, anti-money-laundering and other laws.

## Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only where needed with:

- our website hosting and email providers, who process data on our behalf;
- logistics, inspection, banking and insurance partners when needed to complete a transaction you have asked for;
- professional advisers, and authorities where the law requires it.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as UK adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a contract are kept for up to 24 months. Order, invoice and transaction records are kept for at least six years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as required by UK law. Uploaded photos are kept with the enquiry they belong to.

## Your rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability, subject to legal exceptions. To exercise any of these rights, email [info@scrapbaysltd.com](mailto:info@scrapbaysltd.com). You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)).

## Security

We use HTTPS across the site, limit access to submitted data to staff who need it, and protect our forms against automated abuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send payment card details through our forms or email — we never ask for them.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
