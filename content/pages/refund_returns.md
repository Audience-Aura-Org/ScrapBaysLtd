We trade bulk commodities — scrap metal, ingots, electronic and plastic scrap, rubber and timber — under individually agreed contracts. Because these materials are shipped in bulk and vary by batch, our approach to returns differs from a consumer shop. This policy applies unless your signed contract says otherwise.

## Before shipment

- **Cancellations:** you may cancel an order without charge until we have confirmed it in writing. After confirmation, cancellation is only possible by agreement and may be subject to costs already incurred (for example loading, inspection or freight bookings).
- **Pre-shipment inspection:** we encourage buyers to arrange independent inspection before loading. Material accepted at inspection is considered to meet the agreed specification.

## Quality and quantity claims

If delivered material does not match the grade, composition or quantity confirmed in your contract:

1. Notify us in writing at [info@scrapbaysltd.com](mailto:info@scrapbaysltd.com) **within 7 days of arrival** at the destination, quoting your order reference.
2. Include photos, weighbridge tickets and, where relevant, an independent inspection or assay report.
3. Keep the material available and unaltered until we have reviewed the claim.

We will review every claim in good faith and respond within 10 working days.

## Remedies

Where a claim is accepted, we will agree one of the following with you: a price adjustment, a credit against future orders, replacement material, or a full or partial refund. Refunds are paid by bank transfer to the account that made the payment, normally within 14 days of agreement.

## What is not covered

- Normal variation within the tolerances stated in the contract.
- Claims made after the notification period, or after the material has been processed, mixed or resold.
- Changes of mind once material has been shipped.
- Losses covered by your cargo insurance during transit — please claim through your insurer in that case.
