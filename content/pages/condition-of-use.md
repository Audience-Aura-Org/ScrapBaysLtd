Most materials are sold on a "request a quote" basis. A quote request is not an order. Our quotations are valid for the period stated on them (or 7 days if no period is stated) and are subject to availability and market movements until a contract is confirmed in writing.

## 4. Orders

Where a price is shown on the Site, you may place an order online. Placing an order is an offer to buy; a contract is formed only when we confirm the order in writing (for example by issuing a pro-forma invoice or sales contract). We may decline any order, including where prices or availability were shown in error.

## 5. Prices and payment

Prices are shown in the currency stated and exclude shipping, insurance, duties and taxes unless stated otherwise. We do not take card payments online. Payment is made by **bank transfer against our pro-forma invoice**, or on **invoice terms** for approved accounts, as set out in our confirmation. Always confirm our bank details by phone before making a first payment, and quote your order reference.

## 6. Inspection, delivery and risk

Delivery terms (Incoterms®), loading, inspection arrangements and shipping schedules are agreed for each contract. Unless agreed otherwise, risk passes in accordance with the agreed Incoterm and title passes when we have received payment in full. Third-party pre-shipment inspection can be arranged at the buyer's cost.
