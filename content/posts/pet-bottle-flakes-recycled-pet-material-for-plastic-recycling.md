**PET Bottle Flakes** are recycled polyethylene terephthalate (PET) materials produced by collecting, sorting, cleaning, and processing used PET bottles. They are widely used by plastic recyclers and manufacturers as a raw material for producing new recycled plastic products.

Depending on the processing and sorting method, PET Bottle Flakes can be supplied in different colours, sizes, moisture levels, and quality grades. Clean and properly sorted flakes are suitable for a range of recycling and manufacturing applications.

**Common applications include:**

-   Recycled PET products
-   Plastic sheet and packaging production
-   Fibre and textile manufacturing
-   Strapping and industrial products
-   General PET recycling and reprocessing

Bulk quantities can be supplied according to buyer requirements, with specifications such as colour, flake size, cleanliness, moisture content, and packaging discussed before purchase.
