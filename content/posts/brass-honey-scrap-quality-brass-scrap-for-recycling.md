Brass Honey Scrap is a valuable non-ferrous metal scrap commonly recognised for its distinctive golden-yellow colour and copper-zinc composition. It is widely traded in the metal recycling industry and used as a raw material by brass foundries, metal processors, and recycling facilities.

Properly sorted Brass Honey Scrap can be melted and reprocessed to manufacture new brass products, fittings, valves, plumbing components, and various industrial parts. Its recyclability makes it a useful resource for recovering valuable metals and reducing the need for virgin raw materials.

The material’s quality may vary depending on its composition, cleanliness, and contamination levels. Buyers can discuss required grades, quantities, packaging, and inspection arrangements before shipment.

Brass Honey Scrap is suitable for scrap traders, metal recyclers, foundries, and manufacturers seeking recyclable brass materials for industrial processing and production.
