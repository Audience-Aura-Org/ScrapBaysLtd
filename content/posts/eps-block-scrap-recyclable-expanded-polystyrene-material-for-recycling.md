**EPS Block Scrap** is expanded polystyrene material collected from manufacturing, packaging, construction, and other industrial applications. Known for its lightweight structure and high volume, EPS can be recovered and processed into reusable material for various recycling applications.

Clean and properly sorted **EPS Block Scrap** is particularly suitable for recycling facilities and manufacturers looking for a consistent source of recyclable polystyrene. The material can be compacted, processed, and converted into recycled products, helping reduce plastic waste and support a more circular economy.

EPS blocks may vary in size, density, colour, and condition depending on their original application. Buyers can specify their required quality, packing method, quantity, and technical specifications when sourcing the material.

**Typical applications include:**

-   Recycling and reprocessing
-   Production of recycled polystyrene products
-   Packaging material recovery
-   Industrial plastic recycling
-   Construction-material recovery

For bulk trading, availability and specifications can be discussed according to the buyer’s requirements.
