Aluminum UBC Scrap (Used Beverage Can Scrap) consists of discarded aluminium cans collected from beverages such as soft drinks, juices, and other canned drinks. It is a valuable recyclable material widely used by aluminium recycling facilities, metal processors, and manufacturers.

Properly collected and sorted Aluminum UBC Scrap can be processed, melted, and reused to manufacture new aluminium products, including beverage cans and other metal components. Its recyclability makes it an important resource for reducing waste and supporting a circular economy.

The material may vary in cleanliness, moisture content, contamination levels, and packaging depending on the collection and sorting process. Buyers can specify their preferred grade, quantity, and packaging requirements when sourcing bulk supplies.

Aluminum UBC Scrap is suitable for recycling companies, aluminium smelters, scrap traders, and industrial buyers seeking recyclable aluminium materials for processing and reuse.
