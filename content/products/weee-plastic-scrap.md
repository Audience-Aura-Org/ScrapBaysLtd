[**WEEE Plastic Scrap**](/product/ceramic-cpu-scrap/) is recovered from waste electrical and electronic equipment and provides a valuable source of recyclable plastic materials. It can originate from computers, electrical appliances, electronic housings, office equipment, consumer electronics, and other discarded electrical products.

Properly sorted [**WEEE Plastic Scrap**](/product/computer-hard-drive-scrap/) can contain different plastic types, including ABS, HIPS, PP, PC, PVC, and other engineering plastics. Sorting by polymer type, colour, and quality helps recyclers achieve better processing results and produce consistent recycled materials.

The material is suitable for **plastic recycling, reprocessing, compounding, and manufacturing applications**. Depending on the source, the scrap may be supplied as whole plastic components, separated parts, shredded material, or processed flakes.

For buyers and recycling companies, specifications can be agreed according to the required polymer type, cleanliness, colour, particle size, packaging, and quantity.
